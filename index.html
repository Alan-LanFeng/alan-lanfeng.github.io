About

I am a PhD candidate at EPFL, Switzerland, working with Prof. Alexandre Alahi at the VITA Lab. Before EPFL I received my M.Sc. in Robotics, Systems and Control from ETH Zurich and B.E. in Navigation Engineering from Wuhan University.

I have built synthetic training data for physical AI since before my PhD. My master's thesis at the AIT Lab at ETH Zurich, with Prof. Otmar Hilliges and NVIDIA, trained robot handovers on synthetic hand-object motions. Earlier, with Prof. Bolei Zhou at UCLA, I worked on generative simulation for reinforcement learning.

My current research makes generation itself cheap. RDM trains one-step generators by matching feature distributions under frozen encoders, and turns the four-step FLUX.2 [klein] into a one-step model that beats its teacher. PTD, from my internship at NVIDIA Research, distills the 33B audio-video model MiniMax-H3 into a four-step generator with LoRA alone, and keeps more of its motion and diversity than DMD-style distillation. On the data side, TAROT (ICML 2025) selects training data with optimal transport, and RAP (ICLR 2026) shows that policies can be trained on cheap 3D rasterizations instead of photorealistic renders. I have also worked on minute-long human animation (EverAnimate, NeurIPS 2026) and on world models that let a robot plan toward goals given in language (Grounded World Model).

Next, I am combining these into world models that are fast enough for closed-loop training and that generate the situations a policy fails on. I am actively seeking research roles and collaborations on generative models, world models, and physical AI. Please reach out if our interests overlap.

Generative models

4 steps
Parametric Trajectory Distillation for Few-Step Video Generation
Lan Feng, Peter Karkus, Maximilian Igl, Julius Berner, Yuxiao Chen, Shuhan Tan, Alexandre Alahi, Boris Ivanovic, Marco Pavone
arXiv 2026  ·  w/ NVIDIA Research
Few-step distillation for large video diffusion and flow models: the student fits each denoising segment with a polynomial curve under a single least-squares loss and keeps the teacher's architecture. On the 33B audio-video MiniMax-H3, four-step PTD trained with LoRA alone keeps more motion and diversity than DMD-style distillation, and wins 55.1% and 63.4% of blinded human votes against PDD and LightX2V Turbo.

Representation Distribution Matching for One-Step Visual Generation
Lan Feng, Wuyang Li, Éloi Zablocki, Matthieu Cord, Alexandre Alahi
arXiv 2026
A one-step image generator trained by matching feature distributions under frozen encoders — no online teacher, adversary, trajectory, or JVP. Sets the one-step ImageNet state of the art, and post-trains four-step FLUX.2 [klein] into a single step that surpasses its teacher.

Wuyang Li, Yang Gao, Mariam Hassan, Lan Feng, Wentao Pan, Po-Chien Luan, Alexandre Alahi
NeurIPS 2026
Minute-long human animation without drift: a persistent latent memory carries identity and motion across chunks, and restorative flow matching refines each chunk during sampling, with LoRA tuning only.

World models and data for physical AI

Grounded World Model: Latent Planning with Language Goals
Quanyi Li, Lan Feng, Haonan Zhang, Wuyang Li, Letian Wang, Alexandre Alahi, Harold Soh
arXiv 2026
A latent world model that imagines the outcome of candidate actions and scores it against a language instruction in a shared vision-language space, so a robot can plan toward goals given in words. Solves 87% of 288 tasks with unseen instructions, against 22% on average for ten fine-tuned VLA models.
